02 — Approach

Methodically from problem to scalable solution.

Understand

Context before interface.

Stakeholder interviews, context analysis and journey mapping clarify who uses the product and under which conditions. The outcome is prioritised requirements instead of assumptions.

Validate

Prototypes, not presentations.

Functional prototypes make scenarios testable early. Usability tests in short iteration cycles deliver solid insights before any development effort is spent.

Scale

Systems that hold up.

Design systems with tokens, defined states and a direct link to code keep products, platforms and teams consistent.

Accessibility

WCAG AA as the minimum.

Regular accessibility audits and the related certification shape my work. AI-assisted scripts and Markdown rules check contrast, focus order and structure while still in design – not only at audit time.

Collaboration

Close to product and sales.

Design as a basis for decisions in product management, engineering and sales – with measurable goals instead of matters of taste.
